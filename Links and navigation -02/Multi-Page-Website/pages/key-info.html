Key Information About The Office

Basic Facts

Here are the important facts about The Office TV series:

When Did It Start?

First Episode: March 24, 2005

Last Episode: May 16, 2013

The show ran for 8 years on television.

How Long Was It?

Total Seasons: 9 seasons

Total Episodes: 201 episodes

Each season has between 20 and 26 episodes.

Where Did It Air?

Network: NBC (National Broadcasting Company)

The Office is an American TV show, so it aired on American television.

Who Created It?

Creator: Greg Daniels

Greg Daniels adapted the show from the British version. The original British Office was created by Ricky Gervais and Stephen Merchant.

Where Does It Take Place?

Location: Scranton, Pennsylvania

The show takes place in the Scranton branch office of Dunder Mifflin, a paper company.

What Genre Is It?

Genre: Mockumentary Sitcom

Mockumentary means it is pretending to be a documentary. Sitcom means it is a comedy show.

Was It Successful?

Yes! The Office was very successful. It won many awards and became very popular. Even after it ended, many people still watch it on streaming services.
