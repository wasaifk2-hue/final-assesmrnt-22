Delicious Chocolate Cake Recipe

About This Recipe

Hello! This is my favorite chocolate cake. It is very easy to make at home. Just mix everything together and the cake comes out soft and good. Everyone loves it, try it now!

Time: 15 min | Bake: 30 min | Serves: 8-10

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180C.
  2. Mix dry ingredients in a big bowl.
  3. In another bowl, mix eggs, sugar, milk, oil, and vanilla.
  4. Put the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and mix.
  5. Put some oil in the baking pan.
  6. Pour the mix into the pan.
  7. Bake for 30 minutes.
  8. Cool the cake for 10 minutes.
