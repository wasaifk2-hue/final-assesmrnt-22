The Cast of The Office

Main Actors

These are the main actors who play the most important characters in The Office:

Steve Carell

Character: Michael Scott

Role: Branch Manager

Steve Carell plays the boss of the office. Michael Scott wants to be friends with all the workers. Steve Carell is very funny in this role and won many awards for playing Michael.

Rainn Wilson

Character: Dwight Schrute

Role: Assistant Regional Manager

Rainn Wilson plays Dwight, who is very serious and loyal to Michael. Dwight loves his beet farm and is often the target of jokes from the other workers.

John Krasinski

Character: Jim Halpert

Role: Sales Representative

John Krasinski plays Jim, who is charming and funny. Jim is the main character in the love story with Pam. He is liked by many fans of the show.

Jenna Fischer

Character: Pam Beesly

Role: Receptionist (then Sales Representative)

Jenna Fischer plays Pam, who works at the front desk at the start. Later she moves to sales. Pam is the love interest of Jim, and their relationship is a major part of the show.

B.J. Novak

Character: Ryan Howard

Role: Temp (then Sales Representative)

B.J. Novak plays Ryan, who starts as a temp worker. Ryan is ambitious and often tries to move up in the company. He is known for being arrogant at times.

Mindy Kaling

Character: Kelly Kapoor

Role: Customer Service Representative

Mindy Kaling plays Kelly, who works in customer service. Kelly is funny, dramatic, and loves talking about pop culture and celebrities.

Other Important Cast Members

  • Angela Martin - Played by Angela Kinsey (Accountant)
  • Erin Hannon - Played by Ellie Kemper (Receptionist and Temp)
  • Kevin Brown - Played by Brian Baumgartner (Warehouse Worker)
  • Oscar Martinez - Played by Oscar Nuñez (Accountant)
