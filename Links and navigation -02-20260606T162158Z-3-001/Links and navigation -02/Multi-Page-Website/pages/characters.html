Main Characters in The Office

Michael Scott

Michael is the manager of the Scranton office. He is well-meaning but often makes mistakes. He wants to be friends with all the workers, but sometimes his jokes are not funny. Michael loves his job and tries hard to help the company. He goes through many relationships during the show.

Jim and Pam

Jim and Pam are the central love story of the series. Jim starts as a sales representative. He is charming, smart, and likes to play pranks on Dwight. Pam starts working as the receptionist at the front desk. At first, she is shy, but she becomes more confident as the show goes on. Jim has always had feelings for Pam, and after a long time, they finally get together. Their relationship is one of the most important parts of the show.

Dwight Schrute

Dwight is the Assistant Regional Manager. He is very serious and intense. He is obsessed with his beet farm where he grows beets. Dwight is extremely loyal to Michael, his boss. Jim likes to play pranks on Dwight, like putting his stapler in jello. Despite being different from everyone else, Dwight is liked by many fans.

Ryan Howard

Ryan starts as a temp worker in the office. He is young and ambitious. He wants to become successful and move up in the company. Ryan sometimes acts like he is better than everyone else. He has a complicated relationship with Kelly. Later in the show, he becomes a sales representative.

Kelly Kapoor

Kelly works in customer service. She is funny and dramatic. She loves talking about celebrities, TV shows, and pop culture. Kelly is obsessed with Ryan and always tries to get his attention. She often gets into fights with other people in the office, especially when she is angry.

Angela Martin

Angela works as an accountant in the office. She is very serious and follows the rules. She loves cats very much. Angela is very different from the other workers because she is not funny and does not like jokes. She is often angry at things that happen in the office.

Erin Hannon

Erin starts as a temp and later becomes the receptionist. She is very happy and positive. She loves life and enjoys working at the office. Erin is funny in a crazy way. She sometimes says or does strange things. Everyone likes her because she is always smiling.

Oscar Martinez

Oscar is an accountant who is intelligent and educated. He is one of the smartest people in the office. Oscar often fights with Michael about jokes or things Michael says that he thinks are wrong. He is honest and speaks his mind.

Kevin Brown

Kevin works in the warehouse at the bottom of the office building. He is friendly but not very smart. Kevin loves food and is always eating or talking about eating. He likes working in the warehouse and does not want to move to the office.
